opencode companion

Formerly opencode remote · iPhone app for opencode

Run the opencode AI coding agent on your computer, then follow the work from your phone: watch replies stream in, approve permissions, review diffs and send the next instruction from anywhere. Your code never leaves your machine, and no account is needed.

Download on the App Store

7-day free trial, then one payment. No subscription.

Get started in three steps

npm install -g opencode-ai     # once, if you do not have opencode yet
opencode auth login            # once
npx ocremote --pair            # shows a QR code

Open the app, scan the QR code and you are connected. Keep the terminal open while you use it. Needs Node.js 22.13+ on macOS, Linux or Windows.

What you can do

  • Follow sessions live: streaming replies, reasoning and tool calls.
  • Approve or reject permissions from your phone. Live Activities show every session on the lock screen.
  • Answer the agent's questions without leaving the app.
  • Review every diff the agent produces.
  • Connect over an end-to-end encrypted relay, your local network or Tailscale. No VPN or port forwarding.
  • Version 1.2 adds Allow / Reject buttons on the notification itself (lock screen and Apple Watch) and 33 languages.

Questions

Is there an iPhone app for opencode?
Yes: opencode companion, a native iOS app that connects to opencode running on your own computer.

How do I control opencode from my phone?
Run npx ocremote --pair on your computer and scan the QR code with the app.

Is my code sent to a server?
No. The agent runs on your computer. The relay only routes encrypted frames and cannot read them.

How much does it cost?
A 7-day free trial, then a single one-time payment that unlocks it for good. No subscription.
